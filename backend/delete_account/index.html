Delete your ReceiptAI account

You can permanently delete your account and associated ReceiptAI data directly in the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Open ReceiptAI and sign in.
  2. Open Profile, then Privacy & Security.
  3. Select Delete My Account and confirm your credentials.

This removes your saved receipts, receipt-derived memory, agent conversation data, learned aliases, feedback, and authentication account. The action cannot be undone.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@receiptai.app from the email address registered to your ReceiptAI account. Include only the account email address. Never send your password, authentication token, or receipt images.

We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing a web request.
